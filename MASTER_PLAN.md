# SKYCOIN4444 Standalone Product Master Plan

Product #7: **Sky Identity**.

Sky Identity is the independently deployable identity and authentication service for the SKYCOIN4444 platform. The product target is standards-based authentication, token issuance/validation, secure password handling, account lifecycle controls, health/readiness, auditability, container packaging, and exact-head CI evidence.

It does not claim third-party compliance certification, enterprise SSO federation, hardware-backed signing, or multi-region HA unless those capabilities are implemented and verified.

Future SKYCOIN4444 integration occurs through versioned identity contracts; the standalone repository remains independently maintained.
